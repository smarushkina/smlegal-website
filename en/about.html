About SM Legal

A law firm built to support businesses at every stage of their development.

Our mission

SM Legal aims to support your business entirely and to provide a wide spectrum of excellent legal services by understanding your business activity and goals. We support you in the incorporation process and during each stage of corporate change and growth.

Who our clients are

We work with founders of start-ups and technology companies, investors and venture capital funds, small and medium-sized enterprises, foreign companies entering the Bulgarian market, and individuals — entrepreneurs, managers and highly qualified professionals relocating to Bulgaria.

How we work

Every engagement starts with a clear definition of the task, timeline and fees. We offer hourly rates, fixed fees for defined services and retainer arrangements for clients with ongoing needs.

Team

Attorney-at-law, Founder of SM Legal

Sylvia Marushkina

Sylvia Marushkina specialises in commercial and corporate law, venture capital transactions and intellectual property, and has long-standing experience working with Bulgarian and international clients.

Her practice covers company formation and corporate housekeeping, structuring investment transactions, registering and protecting trade marks in Bulgaria, the EU and under the Madrid System, as well as immigration and employment matters for companies hiring talent from abroad.

Her motivation for professional growth and eagerness to help clients achieve their goals have led to a high level of professionalism, positively assessed by many clients.
