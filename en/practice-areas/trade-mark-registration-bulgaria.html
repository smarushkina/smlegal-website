Trade Mark Registration in Bulgaria

A registered trade mark gives you exclusive rights in your brand name and logo. We handle the whole process — from the clearance search and the choice of classes to the registration certificate.

A trade mark is often a business's most recognisable asset. Without registration, however, rights in it are hard to prove, and a competitor may file a similar sign before you do. Registration with the Bulgarian Patent Office under the Marks and Geographical Indications Act gives you the exclusive right to use the mark for the goods and services covered and to prevent others from using identical or similar signs.

SM Legal supports clients throughout — from the preliminary search and protection strategy to registration, watching and renewal, in Bulgaria, in the European Union and under the Madrid System.

What can be registered

A trade mark can be any sign capable of distinguishing your goods or services from those of others: words and names, logos and images, letters and numerals, colours, three-dimensional shapes, sounds or combinations of these. Signs that lack distinctive character, merely describe the goods or services, have become customary in trade or may mislead consumers cannot be registered.

The procedure step by step

1. Clearance search

Before filing we check whether the sign meets the legal requirements and whether there are earlier identical or similar marks in the Bulgarian register, the EU trade mark register and international registrations covering Bulgaria. A search is the cheapest way to avoid a refusal or an opposition.

2. Goods and services

Goods and services are listed by class under the Nice Classification. A precise specification is essential: too narrow and your business is left unprotected; too broad and fees and the risk of disputes increase. We draft the list around your actual and planned activities.

3. Filing the application

We file the application electronically with the Patent Office. The filing date establishes your priority over later similar signs and starts the term of protection.

4. Examination and publication

The Office examines the formal requirements and the absolute grounds for refusal. Once examination is complete, the application is published in the Official Bulletin.

5. Opposition period

Within three months of publication, owners of earlier rights may file an opposition. If an opposition is filed, we represent you in the proceedings and look for the best outcome — including a settlement with the opponent.

6. Registration and certificate

If no opposition is filed, or it is rejected, the mark is entered in the State Register once the registration fee is paid, and a registration certificate is issued.

Timing and fees

Without objections, the procedure usually takes several months; opposition proceedings can extend it considerably. Official fees are set by the Patent Office tariff and depend mainly on the number of classes. At the first consultation we give you an exact estimate of official fees and our fee.

Bulgarian, EU or international trade mark

A Bulgarian mark protects you in Bulgaria only. An EU trade mark filed with the EUIPO gives uniform protection in all Member States, while the Madrid System lets you seek protection in many countries with a single application. Within six months of your first filing you may claim its priority for applications in other countries. For a detailed comparison, see our article “National, EU or International Trade Mark — How to Choose”.

After registration

A registration is valid for ten years from the filing date and may be renewed indefinitely. The mark must be genuinely used: if it is not used for five years, it may be revoked at the request of an interested party. We also recommend watching new applications so that you can act promptly against similar signs.

Frequently asked questions

How long does trade mark registration take in Bulgaria?

Without objections, the procedure before the Bulgarian Patent Office usually takes several months. If an opposition is filed, it can take considerably longer. We provide a realistic timetable at the first consultation.

How much does it cost to register a trade mark?

Official fees are set by the Patent Office tariff and depend mainly on the number of classes of goods and services. We give you an exact estimate of the official fees and our fee before filing.

Do I need a search before filing?

It is not mandatory but strongly recommended. A search shows whether earlier similar marks could lead to an opposition and saves the cost of an application that would be refused.

How long is a registered trade mark valid?

Ten years from the filing date, renewable indefinitely for further ten-year periods.

Can I extend protection to other countries?

Yes. Within six months of filing in Bulgaria, you can claim its priority for an EU trade mark application or an international registration under the Madrid System.
