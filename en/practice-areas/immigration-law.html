Immigration

We advise EU and third-country nationals on obtaining short-term, long-term and permanent residence in Bulgaria, and support employers in hiring foreign professionals.

We represent our clients before the Migration Directorate, the Employment Agency and consular offices, prepare all relevant documents and submit applications so as to achieve a positive result in the shortest possible time.

We advise on the most suitable regime for each case — the EU Blue Card for highly qualified professionals, the single residence and work permit, intra-corporate transfers, residence based on business activity or investment, and family reunification.

Combining immigration and employment law expertise allows us to plan the process as a whole — from the employment contract and job advertisement to the visa, address registration and renewal of the permit.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an EU Blue Card and a single permit?

The EU Blue Card is designed for highly qualified professionals with higher education or equivalent professional experience and a salary above a set threshold. The single permit is the general employment regime for third-country nationals. The Blue Card usually offers more favourable conditions for changing employers and for family members.

How long does the procedure take?

Timeframes depend on the type of permit and the workload of the authorities. The process usually takes several months, and we provide a realistic timeline at the first consultation.

Do EU citizens need a permit to live in Bulgaria?

EU citizens do not need a work permit, but if they stay longer than three months they must obtain a long-term residence certificate. Their non-EU family members obtain a residence card under a separate procedure.
