Real Estate

We advise on a wide range of real estate matters and provide our clients with creative and efficient solutions — from verifying title to completing the transfer and managing the property.

Real estate transactions require careful review of title documents, encumbrances and the legal status of the property. We carry out thorough analysis before our clients take on any financial commitments.

We draft and negotiate preliminary agreements, draft notarial deeds and lease agreements, protecting the client's interests at every stage — from the deposit to taking possession.

We advise foreign individuals and companies on the rules for acquiring real estate in Bulgaria, including structuring the acquisition through a Bulgarian company where necessary.

Frequently asked questions

Can foreigners buy property in Bulgaria?

EU and EEA citizens may acquire real estate, including land, on the same terms as Bulgarian citizens. Third-country nationals may acquire buildings and individual units, but their acquisition of land is restricted, which is why a Bulgarian company is often used.

What should be checked before buying a property?

Title documents and the history of the property, registered encumbrances — mortgages, attachments, pending claims — the tax valuation, local tax liabilities, building permits and compliance of the property with them, and the seller's marital status.

Is a notarial deed required?

Yes. Ownership of real estate is transferred by an agreement in notarial form, which is recorded in the Property Register.
