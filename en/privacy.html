Privacy Policy

This policy explains how SM Legal — Marushkina Law Firm ("we") processes the personal data of visitors to smlegal.bg and of persons who contact us.

Data controller

Attorney-at-law Sylvia Marushkina, 41 Dobrotitsa Despot Str., Sofia 1612, Bulgaria, email: office@smlegal.bg.

Data we process

We process the data you provide via the contact form or by email — name, email address, phone number, company name and the content of your message.

Purposes and legal basis

  • responding to your enquiry and taking steps prior to entering into an engagement — Art. 6(1)(b) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR);
  • compliance with legal obligations, including under the Bar Act and anti-money laundering legislation — Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR;
  • consent given through the contact form — Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Retention

Data from enquiries that do not result in an engagement are kept for up to 12 months. Client data are kept for the periods required by applicable law.

Recipients

We do not disclose your data to third parties, except to hosting and email service providers acting on our behalf, or where required by law. Attorney–client privilege is observed at all times.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are loaded from the Google Fonts service.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You may exercise your rights by writing to office@smlegal.bg. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commission for Personal Data Protection (www.cpdp.bg).
