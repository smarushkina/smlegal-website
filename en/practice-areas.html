Frequently asked

What is a variable capital company in Bulgaria?

The variable capital company is a legal form introduced into the Bulgarian Commerce Act specifically for innovative start-ups. It allows more flexible admission and exit of shareholders and the issue of different classes of shares, which makes it well suited to attracting investors and to employee option plans.

Who owns code written by a freelancer?

As a general rule, copyright vests in the author of the work. Where software is created under a services contract, the rights must be expressly addressed in the contract. Without a clear clause the company risks not owning key parts of its own product.

Is a written employment contract mandatory in Bulgaria?

Yes. Employment contracts must be concluded in writing, and the employer must notify the National Revenue Agency within the statutory deadline and in any event before the employee starts work.

Immigration

We advise EU and third-country nationals on obtaining short-term, long-term and permanent residence in Bulgaria, and support employers in hiring foreign professionals.

  • Family reunification
  • Type D visa and consular assistance
  • Renewals, amendments and change of employer
  • Appeals against refusals

Frequently asked

What is the difference between an EU Blue Card and a single permit?

The EU Blue Card is designed for highly qualified professionals with higher education or equivalent professional experience and a salary above a set threshold. The single permit is the general employment regime for third-country nationals. The Blue Card usually offers more favourable conditions for changing employers and for family members.

Who is an obliged entity under Bulgarian AML law?

The scope is broad and includes, besides banks and payment institutions, accountants, tax advisers, lawyers in certain transactions, real estate intermediaries, providers of registered office services, virtual asset service providers, high-value goods dealers and others. We recommend an individual assessment of your status.

Frequently asked

Do I need a Data Protection Officer?

The obligation applies to public authorities and where the controller's core activities involve regular and systematic monitoring of individuals on a large scale or large-scale processing of special categories of data. In other cases appointing a DPO is advisable but not mandatory.

Frequently asked

Can foreigners buy property in Bulgaria?

EU and EEA citizens may acquire real estate, including land, on the same terms as Bulgarian citizens. Third-country nationals may acquire buildings and individual units, but their acquisition of land is restricted, which is why a Bulgarian company is often used.
